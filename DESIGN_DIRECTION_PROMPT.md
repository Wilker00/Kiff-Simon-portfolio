# Portfolio transformation prompt — Digital editorial system

Redesign this UX portfolio as a confident digital editorial publication, combining the restraint and navigation clarity of a contemporary AI product site with the tactile energy of experimental Swiss print design.

## Creative direction

- Build a quiet, highly structured interface on warm off-white paper.
- Use black typography, generous negative space, thin rules, and an exact modular grid.
- Introduce a compact primary palette: vermilion red, cobalt blue, warm yellow, forest green, and black.
- Use color as a navigation and classification system, not decoration: each project receives one primary color.
- Add subtle paper grain and controlled pixel/checker fragments inspired by generative systems, computation, and imperfect print registration.
- Keep the composition asymmetric and editorial. Pair large, plain-spoken headlines with small monospaced labels, indices, outcomes, and captions.
- Prefer square corners, flat fields, and crisp borders. Avoid glassmorphism, excessive pills, generic gradients, soft SaaS cards, and decorative 3D effects.

## Homepage

- Keep the introduction direct and text-led.
- Present one signature generative poster beside the hero statement, combining a real project artifact with a raster color field.
- Turn selected work into a horizontal, art-led card rail like an editorial product catalog. Cards should reveal a partial next item on smaller screens and support scroll snapping.
- Keep case-study imagery authentic. Never replace the actual work with invented interface mockups.
- Use a compact secondary project index beneath the featured rail.

## Interior pages

- Treat case studies as long-form editorial stories: large title, offset summary, structured metadata, full-bleed artifact, and clearly separated narrative sections.
- Preserve readable line lengths and create rhythm through rules, whitespace, and alignment rather than containers around every paragraph.
- Make the About page feel like a profile spread and the Contact page like a minimal studio inquiry page.
- Carry the project color system through thumbnails, labels, rules, focus states, and key outcomes.

## Interaction and quality

- Motion should be subtle: image crops shift slightly, arrows advance, and content reveals without obscuring readability.
- Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior, and touch targets of at least 44px.
- Support 320px through large desktop widths without horizontal page overflow. Horizontal overflow is allowed only inside the intentional project rail.
- Keep all routes, copy, local assets, contact behavior, and accessibility labels working.

The final result should feel authored, intelligent, experimental, and calm—a designer’s point of view rather than a portfolio template.
